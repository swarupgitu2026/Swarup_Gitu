Golden Sands and Chariots: Puri & Konark

Published on October 24, 2026

🛕

Standing in front of the 13th-century Konark Sun Temple at dawn leaves you completely awestruck. Designed in the shape of a colossal stone chariot with intricately carved wheels and horses, it stands as a glowing tribute to Kalinga architecture and human ingenuity.

From Sacred Stones to Golden Shores

Just a short coastal drive along the Marine Drive stretch brings you directly into Puri. The sea breeze carries the fragrance of fresh coastal food and temple incense. Watching the waves break on Golden Beach while listening to local tales makes this journey unforgettable.
